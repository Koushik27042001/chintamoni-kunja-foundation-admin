Apple Silicon Evolution

Monumental performance.
Game-changing graphics.

Built on second-generation 3-nanometer technology, M5 Pro and M5 Max bring unprecedented pro capability. Featuring hardware-accelerated mesh shading, real-time ray tracing, and an ultra-fast Neural Engine ready for Apple Intelligence.

M5 Pro
For demanding workflows and heavy multi-stream editing.
  • Up to 14-core CPU with 10 performance cores
  • Up to 20-core GPU with Dynamic Caching
  • Up to 36GB unified memory (150GB/s bandwidth)
  • Support for up to two high-resolution external displays
M5 Max
The pinnacle of workstation computing in a portable design.
  • Up to 16-core CPU with 12 performance cores
  • Up to 40-core GPU delivering desktop-class 3D render
  • Up to 128GB unified memory (546GB/s bandwidth)
  • Support for up to four external displays (including 8K)

PROLED XDR Display

Behold the brilliance.

Extreme Dynamic Range maintains extraordinary brightness for HDR content, delivering deep blacks, spectacular specular highlights, and vibrant, true-to-life color calibration right out of the box.

Versatile Connectivity

The connections that matter.

MacBook Pro packs a powerful array of ports for connecting high-speed peripherals, driving high-resolution displays, and directly offloading SDXC cards.

Thunderbolt 5
Three ultra-fast ports supporting up to 120Gb/s transfer speeds, charging, and DisplayPort 2.1 video output.
HDMI 2.1
Direct output supporting up to 8K resolution at 60Hz or 4K resolution at 240Hz with multichannel audio.
MagSafe 3 & SDXC
Dedicated quick-release fast magnetic charging, high-speed SDXC card reader, and high-impedance 3.5mm headphone jack.
